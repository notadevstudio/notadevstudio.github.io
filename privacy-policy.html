This shared Privacy Policy applies to the apps and website published by NotADevStudio, also known as Not A Dev Studio.

Request deletion of your personal data

For these apps, email contact@notadevstudio.com with the subject Data deletion request. Include the app name and describe the personal data you want deleted. If the app supports accounts, include your account identifier and state whether you want the account and associated data deleted. Do not send passwords. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

Requests concern personal data we hold about you, including support correspondence and account data where applicable. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify the request and locate the data. We will explain the next steps and expected processing time in our response. If a legal obligation requires retention, we will explain the information retained, the reason and the applicable retention period.

For data stored only on your device, such as local game progress and preferences, use Android Settings > Apps > the app > Storage > Clear storage/data. This resets local progress and does not itself delete information held by us or third parties. Contact us for assistance with data processed by service providers. Account deletion applies only to apps that offer accounts; you can request deletion of other personal data without an account.

Google의 관심 기반 광고/광고 개인화 거부 설정과 Huawei OAID 규칙을 존중합니다. 명시적 동의 없이 데이터를 영구 기기 식별자나 개인 식별 정보에 연결하지 않습니다.

이용 목적

서비스 제공·유지·모니터링, 계정 관리(해당 시), 계약 이행, 업데이트·보안 연락, 거부하지 않은 경우 뉴스·혜택 제공, 요청 처리, 사업 이전, 광고 제공·분석(허용 시 개인화), 데이터·추세 분석과 개선에 이용할 수 있습니다.
